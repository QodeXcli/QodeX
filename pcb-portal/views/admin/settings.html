Pricing

Tariffs used for automatic quotes and the public estimator. All values in USD.

How a quote is built

Design fee = base + per-layer × layers + per-cm² × area + per-net × nets + per-pad × pads. Controlled impedance and BGA multipliers apply to the design fee.

Fabrication (fab services only) = (setup + per-cm² × area × quantity × layer factor) × copper factor × (1 + finish extra + colour extra).

Express multiplies the subtotal. The result is rounded up and raised to the minimum order.

With automatic quoting off, new orders wait in "Awaiting quote" until you set a price.
