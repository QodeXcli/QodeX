    Pricing FAQ

    Is the estimate the final price?

    If you upload a board, the price at checkout is computed from the file itself with the same formula. Some complex boards are quoted manually by an engineer before payment.

    What if you can't route my board?

    If the board cannot be routed as placed, we tell you why before routing starts. If we accept the job and cannot deliver, you are refunded.

    Which currencies can I pay in?

    All prices are charged in USD. Your card issuer or PayPal handles conversion from your local currency.
