Trust & security

Your board is your IP. We treat it that way.

A plain-language description of how your files move through QodeX, who can see them, and what we will never do with them.

01

Private storage

Uploads are stored outside any public path under random identifiers and served only through an authenticated, ownership-checked endpoint.

02

Least access

Only your account and the engineer assigned to your order can download your files. Every upload, status change and payment is logged on the order.

03

Offline routing

Routing runs on dedicated machines that are not reachable from the internet. Only finished deliverables are uploaded back to your order.

04

No model training

Customer files, copper geometry and metadata are never used to train or fine-tune AI models. This is written into our terms.

Data flow

Where your file goes

  1. Your browser parses the board locally to show the preview and quote. Nothing is sent until you place the order.
  2. Upload over TLS to private portal storage, attached to your order only.
  3. Engineer workstation retrieves it for routing on offline hardware.
  4. Deliverables are uploaded to your order; you are notified in the portal.
  5. Deletion of order files is available on request once the order is complete.

Controls at a glance

Passwordsscrypt-hashed, never stored in plain text

Export-controlled designs

If your design is subject to export controls (for example ITAR or EAR), contact us before uploading so we can confirm whether and how we can handle it. Do not upload controlled technical data without that confirmation.
